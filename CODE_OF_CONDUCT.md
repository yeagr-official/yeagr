# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

YEAGR aims to be a technically ambitious and welcoming project.

Contributors should:

- engage with ideas in good faith;
- give specific, constructive technical feedback;
- respect different levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and intimidation;
- keep disagreements focused on the work.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may result in comments being removed, participation being restricted, or contributors being blocked from project spaces.

Concerns can be reported privately to **hello@yeagr.com**.

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this standard fairly.
